📚 Forma Negativa — Did Not / Didn't

Objetivo da aula:

Aprender a construir frases negativas no Simple Past utilizando did not ou didn't.

📖 Conteúdo

Para formar frases negativas no Simple Past, utilizamos did not ou sua forma contraída didn't.

Subject + did not + base verb

I did not study.
Eu não estudei.

She didn't go.
Ela não foi.
Depois de didn't, o verbo volta para sua forma original.

❌ She didn't went.
✅ She didn't go.

💬 Exemplos

I didn't watch TV.

He didn't study yesterday.

We didn't go to school.

They didn't play soccer.

✏️ Atividades

1. Transforme em negativa:

I studied English.
2. Corrija:

She didn't went to school.
3. Complete:

They ______ ______ eat breakfast.
