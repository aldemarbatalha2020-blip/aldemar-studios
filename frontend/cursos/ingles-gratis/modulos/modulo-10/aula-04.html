Módulo 10 — Aula 04: Simple Past — Revisão

Objetivo

Revisar como falar sobre acontecimentos que ocorreram no passado.

Exemplos

I studied yesterday.

She visited her grandmother.

They played soccer.

He watched a movie.

Verbos irregulares

go → went

eat → ate

see → saw

have → had

do → did

Negativa

I didn't study yesterday.

She didn't go to school.

Pergunta

Did you study yesterday?

Did he visit his friend?

Prática

Escreva cinco frases sobre algo que você fez ontem.
