Módulo 10 — Aula 06: Vocabulary & Reading

Objetivo

Utilizar o vocabulário estudado durante o curso em um pequeno texto.

Text

My name is Anna. I am a student. I live in Brazil. I study English every day. Yesterday, I visited my grandmother. Today, I am studying English at home. There is a book on my table and there are two notebooks next to it.

Vocabulário

student — estudante

live — morar

every day — todos os dias

yesterday — ontem

today — hoje

home — casa

book — livro

notebook — caderno

Compreensão

1. What is Anna?

2. Where does she live?

3. What did she do yesterday?

4. What is she doing today?

5. How many notebooks are there?
