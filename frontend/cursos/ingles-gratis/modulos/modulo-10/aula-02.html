Módulo 10 — Aula 02: Verb To Be — Revisão

Objetivo

Revisar o uso do verbo to be no presente.

Formas

I am

You are

He is

She is

It is

We are

They are

Exemplos

I am a teacher.

She is happy.

They are students.

Negativa

I am not tired.

He isn't at home.

They aren't ready.

Perguntas

Are you a student?

Is she your friend?

Are they ready?

Prática

Complete as frases utilizando am, is ou are.
