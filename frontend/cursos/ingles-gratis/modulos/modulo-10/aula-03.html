Módulo 10 — Aula 03: Simple Present — Revisão

Objetivo

Revisar o Simple Present para falar sobre hábitos, rotinas e fatos.

Exemplos

I study English every day.

You work in a school.

She studies English.

He plays soccer.

They live in Brazil.

Atenção

Com he, she e it, normalmente acrescentamos -s ou -es ao verbo.

Negativa

I don't study at night.

She doesn't work on Sunday.

Perguntas

Do you speak English?

Does he study English?

Prática

Escreva cinco frases sobre sua rotina utilizando o Simple Present.
