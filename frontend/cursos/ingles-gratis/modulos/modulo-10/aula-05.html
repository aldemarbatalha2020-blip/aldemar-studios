Módulo 10 — Aula 05: Present Continuous — Revisão

Objetivo

Revisar ações que estão acontecendo neste momento.

Estrutura

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

Exemplos

I am studying.

She is reading.

He is working.

We are learning English.

They are playing.

Negativa

I am not sleeping.

She isn't working.

They aren't studying.

Perguntas

Are you studying?

Is she reading?

Prática

Observe o que acontece ao seu redor e escreva cinco frases usando Present Continuous.
