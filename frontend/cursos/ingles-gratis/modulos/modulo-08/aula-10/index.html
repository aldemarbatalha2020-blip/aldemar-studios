📚 Revisão Geral do Módulo 08

Objetivo da aula:

Consolidar perguntas no passado, sequência narrativa, comparação passado/presente e used to.

📖 Conteúdo

Conteúdos estudados

1. Perguntas no passado

What did you do?
Where did you go?
When did you arrive?
Why did you stay home?
2. Sequência

First → Then → Next → After that → Finally
3. Comparação

Past → Present

I used to play soccer.
Now, I play music.
4. Used to

Subject + used to + verb

💬 Exemplos

A: What did you do yesterday?

B: I went to school.

A: What did you do after school?

B: I studied English.

A: Did you watch TV at night?

B: Yes, I did.

✏️ Atividades

1. Complete:

_____ did you go yesterday?
I went to school.
2. Complete:

_____ did you stay home?
Because I was tired.
3. Organize:

Finally — First — Then — Next
4. Complete:

I _____ to play soccer when I was younger.
(used)
5. Traduza:

I used to walk to school.
6. Escreva um pequeno texto, de 6 a 8 frases, sobre como era sua rotina no passado e como ela é hoje.
Preparação para a avaliação:

Revise principalmente:

• Simple Past
• Did / Didn't
• What / Where / When / Who / Why
• Because
• First / Then / Next / Finally
• Past × Present
• Used to
• Leitura e interpretação
