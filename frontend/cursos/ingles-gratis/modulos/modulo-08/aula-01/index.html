📚 Revisão do Passado

Objetivo da aula:

Revisar o Simple Past, was, were, did e expressões de tempo.

📖 Conteúdo

O Simple Past é usado para falar de ações que aconteceram e terminaram no passado.

Forma Exemplo
Afirmativa I studied English.
Negativa I didn't study English.
Interrogativa Did you study English?
I studied yesterday.

I didn't study yesterday.

Did you study yesterday?

To be no passado

I was tired.
They were happy.
She wasn't home.
Were you tired?

💬 Exemplos

Did you watch TV yesterday?

Yes, I did.

No, I didn't.

✏️ Atividades

1. Passe para a negativa:

I played soccer.
2. Transforme em pergunta:

She studied English.
3. Complete:

They _____ happy yesterday.
