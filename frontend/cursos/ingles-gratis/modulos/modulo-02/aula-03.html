📚 1. Contrações

Na língua inglesa é muito comum unir o pronome ao verbo. Essas formas são chamadas de contrações.

🔤 2. Formas

I am → I'm
I’m a student.
You are → You're
You’re my friend.
He is → He's
He’s a teacher.
She is → She's
She’s happy.
It is → It's
It’s good.
We are → We're
We’re students.
They are → They're
They’re friends.

✏️ 3. Atividades

Transforme em contração:

1. I am → ______

2. She is → ______

3. They are → ______

4. We are → ______

5. You are → ______

🎯 4. Desafio

Escreva cinco frases usando contrações.
