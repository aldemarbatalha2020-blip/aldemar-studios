📖 1. Reading — My Neighborhood

My Neighborhood

I live in a small neighborhood. There is a school near my house. There is also a supermarket and a bakery. There are two parks in my neighborhood. The bank is next to the supermarket. The hospital is in front of the school. My neighborhood is quiet and beautiful.

Tradução

Eu moro em um bairro pequeno. Há uma escola perto da minha casa. Também há um supermercado e uma padaria. Há dois parques no meu bairro. O banco fica ao lado do supermercado. O hospital fica em frente à escola. Meu bairro é tranquilo e bonito.

Interpretação

1. Where does the person live?

2. Is there a school near the house?

3. How many parks are there?

4. Where is the bank?

5. Is the neighborhood quiet?

Vocabulário

live = morar

small = pequeno

also = também

quiet = tranquilo

beautiful = bonito
