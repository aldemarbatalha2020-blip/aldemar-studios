Módulo 09 — Aula 09: Reading Practice

Text

Today, Maria is studying English at home. Her brother is watching TV. Her mother is cooking dinner. Maria is reading a book and writing some sentences in English. Everyone is busy.

Vocabulário

today — hoje | home — casa | brother — irmão | mother — mãe | cooking — cozinhando | dinner — jantar | book — livro | sentence — frase | busy — ocupado

Questions

1. What is Maria doing?

2. Where is Maria studying?

3. What is her brother doing?

4. What is her mother doing?

5. Is Maria writing in English?
