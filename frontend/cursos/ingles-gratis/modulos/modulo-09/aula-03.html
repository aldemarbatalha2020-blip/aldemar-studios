Módulo 09 — Aula 03: Present Continuous — Negative

Aprenda a formar frases negativas no Present Continuous.

Estrutura

Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing

I am not sleeping.

He is not working.

They are not playing.

Contrações

is not = isn't

are not = aren't

Prática

Transforme em negativa:

She is studying.

They are playing.
