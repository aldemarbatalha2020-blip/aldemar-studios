📚 Was e Were

Objetivo da aula:

Compreender o uso do verbo to be no passado.

📖 Conteúdo

O verbo to be possui duas formas no passado: was e were.

Subject Past of be
I was
He was
She was
It was
You were
We were
They were
I was tired.
Eu estava cansado.

They were happy.
Eles estavam felizes.

💬 Exemplos

She was at home.

We were at school.

He was tired yesterday.

They were happy.

✏️ Atividades

1. Complete:

I _____ tired.
They _____ happy.
She _____ at home.
2. Traduza:

We were at school.
3. Crie duas frases utilizando was e were.
