📘 Aula 06 — Don't and Doesn't — Negative Sentences

Curso de Inglês Gratuito • Módulo 03

← Voltar aos módulos

🚫 1. Frases negativas

Para formar frases negativas no Simple Present, usamos don't e doesn't.

📐 2. Don't

Usamos com I, You, We e They.

I don't work on Sunday.

You don't study French.

We don't live here.

They don't play tennis.

📐 3. Doesn't

Usamos com He, She e It.

He doesn't work here.

She doesn't study French.

It doesn't work.

⚠️ Atenção

Depois de doesn't, o verbo fica na forma básica.

✏️ 4. Atividade

Complete com don't ou doesn't:

I ______ work on Sunday.

She ______ speak Spanish.

They ______ study at night.

He ______ play basketball.
