📚 1. A regra da terceira pessoa

Com He, She e It, normalmente acrescentamos -s ao verbo.

📐 2. Exemplos

I work.

He works.

I study.

She studies.

I play.

It plays.

🔤 3. Exemplos de verbos

VerbHe / She / It

✏️ 4. Atividade

Complete:

She ______ English. (study)

He ______ soccer. (play)

She ______ to school. (go)

He ______ books. (read)
