📖 1. Reading

A Day in My Life

My name is Anna. I wake up at 6:30 every morning. I have breakfast with my family. Then I go to school. I study English and Mathematics. In the afternoon, I go home and do my homework. I usually watch TV at night. I go to bed at 10 p.m.
