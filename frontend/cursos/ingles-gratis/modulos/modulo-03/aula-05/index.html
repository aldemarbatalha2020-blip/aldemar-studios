❓ 1. Perguntas no Simple Present

Utilizamos Do e Does para formar perguntas no Simple Present.

📐 2. Do

Usamos Do com I, You, We e They.

Do you study English?

Do they work here?

Do we have class today?

📐 3. Does

Usamos Does com He, She e It.

Does he work here?

Does she study English?

Does it work?

⚠️ Atenção

Depois de does, o verbo volta para a forma básica.

Correto: Does she study?

Incorreto: Does she studies?

✏️ 4. Atividade

Complete com Do ou Does:

_____ you speak English?

_____ she study?

_____ they work?

_____ he play soccer?
