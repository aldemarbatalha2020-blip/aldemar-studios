A Day in Sofia's Life

PARAGRAPH 01

Sofia lives in a small city with her mother and her younger brother. She wakes up at six thirty every morning. She gets out of bed, opens the window, and makes her bed. Then she goes to the bathroom and gets ready for school.

PARAGRAPH 02

At seven o'clock, Sofia has breakfast with her family. She usually eats bread, fruit, and eggs. She drinks orange juice, but she does not drink coffee. After breakfast, she takes her school bag and walks to the bus stop near her house.

PARAGRAPH 03

Sofia's classes start at eight o'clock. She studies English, science, history, and mathematics. She likes English because she enjoys reading short stories. During the break, she talks with her friends and sometimes eats a small snack.

PARAGRAPH 04

Sofia arrives home at one thirty in the afternoon. She has lunch with her family and rests for a short time. Then she does her homework. In the afternoon, she also helps her mother at home and plays with her brother.

PARAGRAPH 05

In the evening, Sofia takes a shower and has dinner with her family. After dinner, she watches television or reads a book. She usually prepares her school bag before bed. Sofia goes to bed at ten o'clock because she needs to wake up early the next morning.
